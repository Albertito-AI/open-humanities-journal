CALL FOR PAPERS | FOUNDING ISSUE

The Human Question: A Journal of Humanistic Inquiry invites original scholarship for consideration in its founding issue.

What do ideas, institutions and discoveries mean for the person and for humanity? We welcome work across law, science, politics, technology and culture that develops a clear argument, engages carefully with its sources and explains the human significance of the inquiry.

Contributions
• Research articles: 6,000–10,000 words
• Review and critical essays: 3,000–6,000 words
• Book reviews and shorter commentaries: 1,000–2,500 words

Word limits include footnotes and exclude the abstract and bibliography. Justified departures may be explained in the covering email.

Research articles and scholarly review or critical essays require two independent external reports, normally under double-anonymous review. Book reviews and shorter commentaries ordinarily receive editorial assessment.

Submissions are considered on a rolling basis. Full guidance, author instructions and editorial policies:
https://thehumanquestionjournal.com/call-for-papers/

Submissions and enquiries: editor@thehumanquestionjournal.com

Open access. No submission, review or publication fees.
