Call for papers | The Human Question, second issue

Who gets to correct the record?

Imagine a selection panel correcting an athlete’s score after the team has travelled, or a supplier revising an emissions figure after the buyer has paid for an audit. What can the institution still do for the person who relied on the original decision?

The Human Question: A Journal of Humanistic Inquiry welcomes papers on that question and original submissions across law, science, politics, technology and culture. We welcome evidence from different jurisdictions and languages, including work that challenges an argument in our founding issue.

Research articles run to 6,000–10,000 words, review and critical essays to 3,000–6,000, and book reviews or shorter commentaries to 1,000–2,500. We consider submissions on a rolling basis. There are no submission, review or publication fees.

Send an anonymised editable Word manuscript, a separate title page and a covering email to editor@thehumanquestionjournal.com. The full call gives the abstract, keyword and review requirements.
