# Publication documents: approved standard

Version 1.0, effective 8 November 2026. Albert Tan Sin Hon, President and Editor-in-Chief, approved the visual sample and authorised publication and saving in Google Drive. The earlier hold pending visual approval is superseded.

Eight public guidance PDFs have been rendered and visually checked. The style guide is four pages; the other seven documents are two pages each. The combined handbook contains 18 pages. The full original logo is retained. A page-break defect in the ethics policy was corrected before release. All documents use the confirmed no-fee policy and CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 article licence.

On 8 November 2026 the owner requested PDF-only distribution. Word downloads were withdrawn, the guide and author instructions were revised to refer to the PDF specimen, and the 18-page handbook was rebuilt. Editable production intermediates are excluded from the public distribution.
