# Publication documents: approved standard

Version 1.0, effective 8 November 2026. Albert Tan Sin Hon, President and Editor-in-Chief, approved the visual sample and authorised publication and saving in Google Drive. The earlier hold pending visual approval is superseded.

Eight Word documents and their PDFs have been rendered and visually checked. The style guide is four pages; the other seven documents are two pages each. The combined handbook contains 18 pages. The full original logo is retained. A page-break defect in the ethics policy was corrected before release. All documents use the confirmed no-fee policy and CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 article licence.

See [the operating standard](OPERATING_STANDARD.md) for production, editorial and change-control requirements. A blank declaration is a form, not an executed publication agreement. Publication receipts belong in the repository history and deployment record.
