# Journal launch checklist

This checklist supports a static journal website and an email-led editorial process. It does not require an online submission system. Unchecked items remain to be verified.

## Prepare the public pages

- [x] Supply and verify the editorial email address before publishing submission instructions: `editor@thehumanquestionjournal.com`, delivery tested and confirmed by Albert on 8 November 2026.
- [x] Publish the President and Editor-in-Chief, ownership, editorial contact and journal aims.
- [ ] Publish confirmed editorial board details as appointments are made.
- [ ] Publish the adopted author instructions, peer-review model, publication schedule, fees or absence of fees, rights/licensing policy and complaints/corrections route.
- [x] Publish the approved house style guide and specimen as PDFs. The specimen is labelled as a template.
- [ ] Test the public pages, resource downloads, article links and layout on desktop and mobile.
- [x] Link the journal from Albert’s official website and verify the live domain, `thehumanquestionjournal.com`.

## Establish the email process

- [ ] Confirm who monitors the editorial mailbox, acknowledges submissions and records manuscript versions.
- [ ] Protect the mailbox, restrict access appropriately and establish backup/retention arrangements.
- [ ] Agree reviewer invitations, conflict checks, report handling and the journal's review model.
- [ ] Assign an independent handling editor for every owner/editor-authored paper.
- [ ] Retain the actual review reports, revisions, author responses and reasoned editorial decision for each paper.
- [ ] Provide email routes for author withdrawal, complaints and appeals.

## Publish each accepted paper

- [ ] Verify acceptance, authors' publication permissions and any third-party permissions.
- [ ] Format the accepted manuscript using the agreed house style without silently altering its substance.
- [ ] Check author order, affiliations, title, abstract, keywords, footnotes, references, figures and page order against the approved manuscript.
- [ ] Obtain the author's approval of the final proof.
- [ ] Add the actual copyright holder, agreed open licence and publication details consistently to the PDF and article page.
- [ ] Include only identifiers actually assigned. Do not invent an ISSN, DOI, volume, issue or citation record.
- [ ] Publish the approved PDF with stable links and verify reading, download and text search.
- [ ] Keep a separate backup of the approved PDF and its metadata. Establish a practical preservation plan.
- [ ] Record later corrections with a dated notice and links to the affected version.

## Singapore ISSN workstream

The ISSN International Centre says national centres serve publishers located in their country. Its general guidance states that online resources are usually assigned an ISSN after publication and identifies five articles as a minimum complete issue for a new publication. Confirm the current Singapore evidence and publication thresholds with NLB before relying on a timetable. Neither this site nor a particular publishing platform guarantees approval.

- [ ] Confirm the publisher's name, Singapore address and exact journal title and medium.
- [ ] Register or access the publisher's NLB Deposit Portal account.
- [ ] Confirm NLB's current online-journal requirements and prepare the required live content and supporting documents.
- [ ] Submit the application and retain the receipt. Display the ISSN only after assignment.
- [ ] Confirm and fulfil applicable legal-deposit obligations.
