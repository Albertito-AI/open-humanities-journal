How a paper reaches publication

Submission, correspondence and peer review take place by email. The website holds journal information and the published archive.

  1. 01

    Email your manuscript

    Send the manuscript, title page and a short covering email to the confirmed submission address.

  2. 02

    Editorial assessment

    The editors consider fit, contribution and readiness for external review. The handling editor must be independent of the authors.

  3. 03

    External peer review

    Suitable papers are sent to independent reviewers. The editorial team manages the review and communicates its decision by email.

  4. 04

    Revision and author approval

    Authors respond to the reviews and revise their papers. Accepted work is prepared in the journal’s house style and returned for proof approval.

  5. 05

    Publication in the open

    The approved PDF and its article details are added to the journal archive for readers to access and share.

Before you send

Your submission package

  • A manuscript with a clear title, abstract and keywords.
  • A separate title page with author names, affiliations and contact details.
  • ORCID identifiers, where available.
  • Complete and consistent citations, footnotes and references.
  • A short explanation of the paper’s contribution and its relevance to humanistic inquiry.
  • Relevant interests, funding and assistance disclosed, with all authors’ agreement to submit.

This is founding guidance. Final editorial policies, submission requirements and any fee arrangements will be stated before submissions open.

Language & argument

Clarity before ornament.

Use British English, informative headings and a clear account of the paper’s question, approach and contribution. Explain specialist terminology for readers beyond your discipline.

Distinguish evidence, interpretation and speculation. Quotations, factual claims and references should be accurate and traceable.

References & sources

Make the evidence accessible.

Use a consistent citation system appropriate to your discipline. Supply complete references and stable links or identifiers where available.

The editors will confirm discipline-specific conventions before the first issue. Legal materials should identify the relevant authority and pinpoint the passage relied upon.

Tables & figures

Let the material do the work.

Number tables and figures, give them informative captions and identify their sources. Explain abbreviations. Include descriptive text for visual material and obtain any required permissions.

Final publication

A proof the author has checked.

After acceptance, the editors prepare a uniform PDF and ask the authors to check it. Publication follows approval of the final text, author details and applicable licence.
